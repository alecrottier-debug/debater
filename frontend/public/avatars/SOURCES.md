# Avatar image sources

All avatars are sourced from Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons, center-cropped
to 512×512 PNG via ImageMagick. Images used here fall under one of:

- **Public domain** (typical for pre-1928 works, U.S. federal government
  works, and many official portraits)
- **CC BY-SA** or **CC BY** — attributed here to satisfy the license

If you believe an image below is incorrectly licensed or attributed, please
open an issue.
